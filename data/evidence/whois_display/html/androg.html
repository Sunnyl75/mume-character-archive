Andróg The Outlaw of Gaurwaith is a
    Maia (Shaper).
Living his life as an outlaw, forever loyal to Turin Turambar, his mortal
life ended in the battle of Amon Rudh as he protected the life of Turin.
For his bravery and fearless behavior he was gifted immortality by the
Valars. Nowadays he spends his time on the shores of Aman, reminiscing
about his bygone mortal life  far  far  in the East.

1187 kits and counting (one day I will catch you Gruenthar!)
