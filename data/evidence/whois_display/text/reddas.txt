Reddas (Foris Zecht), the Judge Magister is a Maia.

A Judge Magister there was... two years past, he took in his hand the Midlight
Shard, stolen from Nabradia, and used it not knowing what he did... and Nabudis
was blown away. Cid ordered this of him to learn the nethicite's true power.
That man swore never to let such terrible power be used again. He forsook his
Judicer's plate, and his name.
