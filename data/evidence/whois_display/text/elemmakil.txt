   Elemmakil was a Noldorin Elf, the captain of those who guarded the outer
entranceway to Gondolin. Here, he encountered Tuor and Voronwë, journeying
to the land at the command of Ulmo. Elemmakil was their guide through the
Seven Gates that guarded Gondolin, until he gave them into the keeping of
his own captain, the Warden of the Great Gate, Ecthelion of the Fountain.
