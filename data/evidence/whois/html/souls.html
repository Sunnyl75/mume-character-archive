Originally a Maia in the service of Námo, Durmegil joined Melkor in Utumno, for
the Dark Lord had promised to allow him to venture forth and collect the souls
of the dead for him. When Utumno was sacked and destroyed by the Valar, rather
than face their judgement, Durmegil fled formless into the far reaches of the
Arda, drawn to the places of the dead.  As such, he spent the most of the First
and Second Ages of the World drifting through the deep, dark places of Arda.

At the rise of Sauron, his presence was sensed by Olórin as he infiltrated the
dungeons of Dol Guldur, where Durmegil had been drawn by the presence of the
Nazgûl. Though his spirit had been darkened by years of walking the paths of
the dead, Olórin sensed that Durmegil was not evil, and so counselled him to
travel into the West and seek the pardon of the Valar.  Námo agreed that he
could resume his service if he would travel to Middle-Earth as an emissary,
helping the Free Peoples, and destroying the minions of Sauron.
