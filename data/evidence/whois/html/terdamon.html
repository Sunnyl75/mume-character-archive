Terdamon the mole of the Dungeon Digging Team is a Maia (Builder)
    [Retired].
Terdamon was a wandering priest of Arda. Travelling from one place to another,
he was always looking for those who were in need of help. He was trying to
reduce the sorrow that weights upon the simple folks, hoping that one day
there will be an Arda without war and suffering for the innocent.
Recently, he seems to have ascended to a higher form of existence. First being
happy about his ascension he had to realize too soon that even this form
hold not enough strength to banish war and suffering from Arda.
