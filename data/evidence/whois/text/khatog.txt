Khatog Kheledhras, emissary of Mahal is a Maia.
Last login 18 years, 6 months, and 14 days ago from *.net.
Khatog was born in the Blue Mountains, and he spent all his mortal
life as an ideal dwarf. He wielded a mighty hammer, and above all loved
the crafts of mining and smithing. He was always willing to help out fellow
Dwarves, as well as anyone else who he befriended. After many years of mortal
life, he finally grew weary of the world and sailed into the west, where he was
made one of the Maiar of Mahal, and his spirit immortalized. Several of his
friends remained in the mortal world, and to this day they follow his path:
Mabzur, Flëgel, Jonin and Ritich.
